Instructions

  • Play in 2 teams (1 to 10 robots per team) or Separately (2 to 10 robots in a free-for-all).
  • Choose from all 50 robot designs for each team or individual contender.
  • 2 players share a keyboard. Left / Right: move · Up / Down: aim · Enter: jump.
  • Hold Space to charge, release to fire · 1: Previous weapon · 2: Next weapon (or click a weapon/object with the mouse).
  • Wind pushes rockets. Terrain breaks. Avoid the water. Supply crates heal. Desktop keyboard required.
  • Land mine throws a proximity mine using your aim angle and charge power; it detonates when any robot gets close.
  • Poison bottle throws a toxic flask using your aim angle and power; hitting a robot poisons it so it loses -1 life every 3 seconds during its turn.
  • Energy bottle throws a bubbly healing flask using your aim angle and power; hitting a robot restores +20 life.
  • Sticky bottle throws a bubbly flask that bursts into white flashing liquid on impact; robots walking through it spend 20% more energy.
  • Rest lays the robot down for 2 seconds, gives +10 life and +10 energy (bars blink), then ends the turn.
  • Wooden wall places a sturdy plank barrier that blocks robots and shots, takes 2 hits to break, and can stack on another wooden wall.
  • Magnet throws a heavy magnet using your aim angle and charge power; once it lands, it pulls nearby robots toward it for 2 seconds before disappearing.

Choose one design. Select another card to replace it.
